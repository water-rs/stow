Usage statistics

The cache, in public.

Anonymous aggregates the installs themselves report: each CLI sends at most one counter report a day and the edge observes the fetches that miss the platform cache, and installs are counted per day by a daily-salted hash that cannot be tied to you. Exactly what is collected — and how to opt out — is in PRIVACY.md. The same numbers are served as JSON at /api/v1/stats.

Usage statistics

The cache, in public.

Anonymous aggregates from requests the CLI already makes: hit events are sampled one in ten and scaled back up, and installs are counted by a daily-salted hash that cannot be tied to you. Exactly what is collected — and how to opt out — is in PRIVACY.md. The same numbers are served as JSON at /api/v1/stats.
